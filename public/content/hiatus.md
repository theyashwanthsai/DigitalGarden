# Where I've been since March

*Written on Oct 12, 2026*

It has been about seven months since I was last active on this site. I took a small hiatus, because a lot happened. This will be a short post covering what happened since then, and what I'm doing now.

## March

My cat passed away on March 17th.

For the ten days before that, I was going to the hospital every morning and evening to get her drips, and then coming back home. It was a very sad time. She finally left on the morning of the 17th.

After that I was numb. I kept working around, trying to stay distracted.

Five days later, I went to Kashi. It was my first ever solo trip, without my parents. I stayed there for three days, and when I came back, I felt like a fresh new version of myself.

## June

I kept working until June, when my company paused its operations. They wanted to reconsider what they were building. That meant I had no work, and my salary was paused too.

So I spent the next few months exploring what I actually want to do. I looked at applied AI for SMBs, applied AI for enterprise companies, and AI for engineering teams. I did a bit of consulting here and there.

From all of that, I understood one thing about AI engineering. Not many people are ready for production grade problems. The exposure just isn't there, especially in the Indian companies and startups that I see.

So I built [Harness Dojo](https://harnessdojo.com), a platform that will help you bridge the gap between what you know and what is actually happening at the edge of this field.

## Evals and RL environments

I was doing a lot of evals during this time. From there, I'm getting back into RL, which I was doing before.

I think RL environments are the way to go. I realised this a bit late, because my job kept me occupied. But most of the intelligence we see in today's models comes from RL environments. So I'm dedicating time to that as well.

## Turi Labs

After May, I also stopped being active at Turi Labs. The community sort of died. Since then, I have been working on a pivot. The goal is simple: I want to understand what is happening in this era, and what it actually asks of you.

That has been my whole ideology for these eight months.

## The next three months

October is almost half done, so I have October, November and December.

My goal is to build something really useful for people in the AI space with Harness Dojo, and at the same time evolve myself. I want to write really good pieces and really good articles. I want to be on top of my game.

That is what harness engineering is for me, and it also covers evals and RL environments. Harness Dojo is a commercial project. Apart from it, I also want to build a bunch of open source projects, just for fun. Side quests.

That's everything I know I want to do.

Thanks for reading.
